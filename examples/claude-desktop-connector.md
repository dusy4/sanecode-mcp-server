# Claude.ai and Claude desktop (custom connector)

Inside the Claude accounts SaneCode runs you don't need this: they get the tools from SaneCode
already. Use it for Claude.ai in a browser, on your phone, or a Claude desktop outside SaneCode.

1. Settings → Connectors → **Add custom connector**.
2. Name: `SaneCode`. URL: `https://mcp.sanecode.co/mcp`.
3. Connect, sign in on sanecode.co with your SaneCode account and press **Allow**.
4. Ask something about your accounts. The first time, SaneCode on your PC asks too
   (Settings → Tools for Claude: Allow).

On Team and Enterprise an owner adds the connector under Organization settings → Connectors.
Needs SaneCode Pro, and *Use SaneCode from other apps* turned on in SaneCode on the PC.

Full guide: [use SaneCode from ChatGPT, Claude.ai and other AI apps](https://sanecode.co/docs/use-sanecode-from-chatgpt-claude-and-other-ai-apps/).
Anthropic's steps: [custom connectors using remote MCP](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11175166-get-started-with-custom-connectors-using-remote-mcp).
